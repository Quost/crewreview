# Crewreview name and logo

The Crewreview source code is free software under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3](LICENSE). The **name "Crewreview", the Crewreview logo and icon, and the crewreview.dev website** are not covered by that license: they identify the official project made by Matheus Quost.

## You can

- Say that your project is based on Crewreview, or that it works with Crewreview ("a fork of Crewreview", "built on Crewreview").
- Use the name and logo to talk about Crewreview: articles, reviews, videos, tutorials, lists of tools.
- Build Crewreview from source for yourself or your team, with the name and logo, as long as you don't distribute it or offer it to others as a service.

## Please don't

- Distribute a modified version, a fork or a repackaged build, or offer one as a hosted service, under the name Crewreview or with its logo. Give it another name and logo so nobody confuses it with the official project.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the official project endorses or makes your product.
- Register domains, app names, accounts or trademarks that include "Crewreview" for a different product.

## Official downloads

Official builds are published only on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Quost/crewreview/releases), through the package managers listed in the README, and on crewreview.dev.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
